About Muneerat

Nurturing minds and building character since 1984.

Our Journey

Muneerat Islamic and Arabic Revival College was founded in 1984 as a dedicated center for Islamic and Arabic education. Starting with just twenty students at Ojuolape, the school has grown into a thriving institution serving hundreds of students across multiple academic levels.

Our Commitment

We provide quality Islamic education combined with modern academic excellence. The institution places weight on character development, moral values, and intellectual growth, preparing students to lead in their communities and contribute meaningfully to society.

The Institution Today

Our campus at Ire Akari Ajape, Modakeke, Osun State, features well-equipped classrooms, dedicated learning spaces, and grounds suited to student activities and congregational prayer.

Our Founder and Leader

Guided by decades of Islamic education and community service

Imam Hadiyyatullahi Ilyas

Founder and Visionary Leader

Imam Hadiyyatullahi Ilyas is the founder of Muneerat Islamic and Arabic Revival College. With a deep commitment to Islamic education and community development, he established this institution as a center for Islamic scholarship and academic excellence.

Beyond his role as founder, Imam Hadiyyatullahi Ilyas serves as the Chief Imam of Modakeke Central Mosque in Gbalefefe, Modakeke, Osun State, where he provides spiritual guidance to the community. He also serves as Chief Missioner of the Nadeerul Islam Society of Nigeria, working to promote Islamic knowledge and understanding.

His lifelong dedication to Islamic propagation, education, and community service remains the foundation upon which Muneerat continues to grow, producing graduates who embody Islamic values alongside academic excellence.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive Islamic and academic education that develops morally upright, intellectually capable, and socially conscious individuals equipped to serve their communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence where Islamic values and modern knowledge are woven together, producing graduates who lead in faith, character, and service to humanity.
